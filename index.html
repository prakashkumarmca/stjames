About Our School

A small beginning with a very big vision

St. James Matriculation School was established in 2010 with a clear vision — to provide quality education to students from rural areas, equal to city standards.

We began as a small institution with just 47 students, and have grown steadily through dedication and commitment. The school faced real challenges during the COVID period, with a temporary decline in student strength. With a well-organised system and the strong support of parents and staff, it has made a remarkable comeback and is now growing rapidly.

Today we focus on quality education, individual attention and the overall development of every student. With dedicated teachers and a clear vision, St. James is emerging as one of the leading institutions in the area.

Our Vision

Our motto, “Our Utmost for the Highest,” inspires us to strive for excellence in all that we do. We envision providing high-quality education to rural students at par with city standards, ensuring equal opportunities for every child.

With a strong commitment to “reaching the unreached,” we aim to empower students through knowledge, values and holistic development.

Our Mission

  • Provide quality education to rural students, equal to city standards
  • Strengthen basic learning, concepts and language skills
  • Offer individual attention and support for every child
  • Create a disciplined, value-based, student-friendly environment
  • Empower every student with confidence, knowledge and life skills

Examination & Assessment

We follow a structured assessment system to monitor and improve student performance. Our evaluation focuses on continuous improvement, concept understanding and the academic progress of every student.

Assessment Includes

1Periodic Slip TestsShort, frequent checks on understanding.
2Unit TestsChapter-wise consolidation.

Examinations

3Mid-Term Exams (I, II, III)Progress checkpoints across the year.
4Term Exams (Term I, II, III)Followed by a Parent-Teacher Meeting.

Student Council

The Student Council gives students the chance to take up leadership roles and responsibilities — developing leadership, discipline, teamwork and confidence, and preparing them to become responsible individuals.

Parent-Teacher Meeting

PTMs are conducted after each Term Examination to discuss student progress and performance, strengthening communication between parents and teachers.
